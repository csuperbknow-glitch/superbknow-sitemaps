AVP vs BELAY

Both companies provide outsourced support to US businesses, but they're built on different models. BELAY is known for matching you with a dedicated individual assistant. AVP builds managed operational teams with structured roles. Here's how to decide which fits your business.

At a Glance

When BELAY May Be the Better Fit

  • You want one dedicated assistant to handle your personal inbox, calendar and travel.
  • You're happy to direct the work yourself and mainly need an extra pair of hands.
  • You also want bookkeeping or website support from the same provider.

When AVP Is the Better Fit

  • You need operations run, not just tasks completed: systems built, maintained and improved.
  • Your sales pipeline and CRM need consistent follow-up and management.
  • You're tired of retraining and want processes that don't depend on one person.
  • You want accountability to sit with a managed team, not with you.
  • You're scaling and need capacity that grows without another hiring cycle.

Questions to Ask Any Virtual Assistant Provider

Whichever provider you're considering, these questions show quickly how the service really works:

  1. Who manages the work day to day: you, or the provider?
  2. What happens when my assistant is sick, on vacation or leaves? Is there coverage, and who handles the handover?
  3. Are processes documented? Who owns the documentation if we part ways?
  4. How is performance measured and reported to me?
  5. Can the support grow into more roles or functions without starting over?
  6. What are the contract terms: minimum hours, notice period, setup fees?
  7. How is my data secured and who has access to my systems?
