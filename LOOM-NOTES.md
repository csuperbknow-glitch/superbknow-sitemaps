# Loom Notes: AVP Website Updates

About 4–5 minutes. **Show** = what's on screen. **Say** = talking points (put them in your own words).

---

## 1. Intro (15 sec)

**Show:** Homepage, top of the page

**Say:**
- "Quick walkthrough of the updates I've made to the AVP website."
- "Three things: the social icons in the footer, the background animation, and a new Blog page I'd like your feedback on."

---

## 2. Social icons in the footer (30 sec)

**Show:** Scroll slowly down to the footer. Hover over each icon.

**Say:**
- "I've added our social media icons to the footer on every page: LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube and Instagram."
- "Each one opens our profile in a new tab, so visitors don't leave our website."
- "They pop in with a small animation when you scroll down, and light up blue when you hover."
- "They're styled to match the rest of the site: the same blue and the same round icon style."

---

## 3. Background lines and loading speed (45 sec)

**Show:** Homepage hero. Leave it still for a few seconds so the moving lines are visible.

**Say:**
- "You'll notice the subtle moving network lines in the background. It fits our 'connected team' idea and makes the site feel more alive."
- "I set it up so it won't slow the site down:"
  - "It only starts after the page has fully loaded, so it never delays what visitors see first."
  - "It only draws what's on the screen, not the whole page, so it's light on the browser."
  - "If someone's device is set to reduce motion, the lines stay still."
- "I also made the site load faster overall:"
  - "The fonts and the big background image now start loading right away."
  - "The logo image is now about a third of its old size."

---

## 4. Blog page: the design (2 min)

**Show:** Open the Blog preview link. Scroll slowly from top to bottom.

**Say (overview):**
- "This is the proposed Blog page. Nothing's live yet, so I'd like everyone's feedback before we publish."
- "It uses the exact same fonts, colors and buttons as the rest of the site, so it feels like part of our website, not a separate template."

**Top section** (point at it)
- "At the top: one big featured story, a headline, and three smaller story links on the right."
- "The 'Read Featured Story' button goes straight to our most important post."

**01. Latest Articles**
- "This is the main list of articles, newest first."
- "These buttons filter by topic: Operations, Virtual Assistants and Growth." *(Click one to demonstrate.)*
- "The newest post is always highlighted in blue so it stands out."

**Stats row**
- "This row highlights our numbers."
- ⚠️ "**These numbers are placeholders.** I need the real figures from the team, or we remove this row."

**02. Expert Guides**
- "This section is for longer, more in-depth guides and playbooks."

**Article page** (click any article)
- "Each article has a clear structure: title, cover image, a short intro, sections, quotes, a key takeaways box, and a 'Contact Us' button at the end."
- "So every post naturally leads readers to book a call."

**Mobile** (optional: shrink the browser window or show it on your phone)
- "It also works on phones: everything stacks into one column."

---

## 5. How we add new posts (30 sec)

**Say:**
- "Adding a post is simple and doesn't need a developer:"
  1. "Copy the blog template file and give it the post's name."
  2. "Replace the text and images. Everything we need to change is clearly marked."
  3. "Add a card for it on the Blog page and upload."
- "I've written a step-by-step guide for whoever will manage the blog."

---

## 6. What I need from the team (30 sec)

**Say:**
- "To move forward, I need:"
  1. ✅ "Approval of the Blog design, or any changes you'd like."
  2. 📊 "Real numbers for the stats row, or OK to remove it."
  3. ✍️ "Our first 3–6 blog topics, and who'll write them."
  4. 📷 "Real photos, if we have any. Otherwise I'll use professional stock images."
- "Once approved, I'll publish it and add the Blog link to the main menu."
- "Thanks! Leave your comments right here on the Loom."

---

### Quick checklist before recording
- [ ] Homepage open, scrolled to the top
- [ ] Blog preview link open in a second tab (Netlify site set to **public**)
- [ ] An article page ready to click into
- [ ] Browser zoom at 100%, other tabs closed
